## E. Earnings quality: the tax reversal

- Note 35.1 (unconsolidated p38; consolidated p27 of the first file): "Effect of prior year charge (50,761,609)". Management reversed a tax provision of Rs 50,762 million relating to prior years, after the appellate tribunal decided against the company on a tax demand; the tribunal decision (ACIR, 17 March 2026) and an ADRC order (8 August 2026) are described, with the company assessing a further appeal.
- Consequence: FY2026 taxation of 16,761,223 includes this credit. Excluding it, the FY2026 tax charge would be 67,522,832 (26.1% of profit before tax), not 16,761,223 (6.5%). Profit for the year excluding the prior-year effect would be 191,612,037, not 242,373,646. This is a material, non-recurring item and the earnings figure should not be read as the run-rate.
- The cash-tax picture is different: income taxes paid were 127,075,622 in FY2026, which is above the underlying tax charge of 67.5bn. The gap between cash tax and the P&L charge is consistent with the disputes described in note 35, but the notes do not reconcile it, and it is recorded as unresolved.

## G. Receivables and circular debt

- **Trade debts:** 594,845,141 at 30 June 2026 against 613,660,983 at 30 June 2025 (down 3.1%), while revenue rose 12.0%. Days of receivables fell from about 558 to about 483 on closing balances (calculated, closing-balance proxy).
- **Overdue trade debts:** note 21.1 (unconsolidated p32; first file p21) states trade debts include an overdue amount of Rs 508,170 million (2025: Rs 549,976 million). This is the figure cited in the Directors' Report (p65 of the coverage register). It is consistent with the KAM on overdue receivables.
- **Overdue related-party debts:** Rs 241,469 million (2025: Rs 231,980 million) are overdue from related parties (note 21.1, unconsolidated p32).
- **Circular debt:** Rs 101,013 million (2025: Rs 140,895 million) relating to inter-corporate circular debts (note 21.x / 39, consolidated p59). Of the overdue amount, Rs 7,208 million (2025: Rs 30,226 million) is overdue on account of inter-corporate circular debt (note 21.1).
- **Ageing:** the trade-debt ageing table (note 39.1.3, unconsolidated pp41–42) is present, but its columns are interleaved in the text layer. Totals by ageing bucket were **not** verified and need manual reading of the page images. The Directors' Report figure for overdue balances is cited, not recalculated.
- **ECL:** the company states that no impairment allowance is necessary for trade debts past due other than the amount provided (note 21.1). The trade-debt impairment allowance moved from 78,527 to 37,422 (reversal 41,105).

**Assessment:** the receivable balance is falling in days terms, but the overdue stock (508 bn) remains very large relative to revenue (449 bn), and the allowance is small. Whether recoverability is improving cannot be concluded from the accounting policy alone; the cash collected from overdue balances is not disclosed in these pages.
