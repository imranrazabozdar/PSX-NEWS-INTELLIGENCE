## What to supply for each sector (owner decision)

For each sector, one recent annual report and one recent quarterly statement, provided as local PDFs in the owner's vault
(not the repository), with the download date if known. Then the statements are verified against the page images, as for
OGDC. Priority companies: HBL or UBL (banks); a listed insurer (none is in the seed, so the owner must name one);
PSO (marketing); HUBC (power); LUCK or KOHC (cement); SYS (technology); EFERT or FFC (fertiliser); a textile composite
company (none in the seed).

## Synthetic test-data requirements (until real documents exist)

Synthetic statements must be labelled synthetic, must never be presented as company data, and must test the logic only:
- identities (assets = liabilities + equity; gross profit; PBT to PAT; cash movement);
- period consistency (quarterly against quarterly; annual against annual);
- consolidated and unconsolidated isolation;
- missing values, dashes and division by zero;
- sector applicability (banks never receive current or inventory ratios; insurers never receive current ratios);
- hand-calculated ratio values.

## Ratio applicability

The complete matrix is generated by `engines/sector_applicability.py` (`matrix_rows()`), with classes applicable,
conditional, not applicable. Conditional measures are never reported as calculable without their stated condition.
