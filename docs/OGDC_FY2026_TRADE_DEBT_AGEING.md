## 5. Movement decomposition and cash-flow check

- Total trade debts: 613,739,510 → 594,882,562 = **−18,856,948**.
- Related-party trade debts: 535,463,628 → 544,675,611 = **+9,211,983**.
- Non-related trade debts: 78,275,882 → 50,206,951 = **−28,068,931**.
- Sum: −18,856,948 ✔.
- The cash-flow statement line "Trade debts" in the operating working-capital section shows **18,856,947** for 2026 (21,371,188 for 2025). This agrees to the balance decrease within Rs 1 thousand, so the cash-flow line is the balance movement, not a separate collection measure.

Conclusions from the decomposition:
1. The reduction in total trade debts is entirely in non-related-party balances. Related-party balances increased by Rs 9.2bn.
2. The non-related over-90 bucket fell by Rs 29.3bn, the largest single movement. The 2026 over-90 balance of Rs 8.3bn is small, but this is the bucket where the decline occurred.
3. Whether the fall reflects collections, write-offs, reclassification or offsetting is **not determinable from this note**. No write-off or reclassification line is disclosed here. The cash-flow line is a net balance movement, not gross collections.

## 6. Days-based ratios (arithmetic; formulas stated)

Receivable days (closing gross trade debts ÷ revenue × 365), revenue from the audited consolidated statements: 2026 = 449,190,752; 2025 = 401,177,969.

- 2026: 594,882,562 ÷ 449,190,752 × 365 = **483.4 days**.
- 2025: 613,739,510 ÷ 401,177,969 × 365 = **558.3 days**.
- Apparent improvement: about 75 days, while revenue grew 12.0%.

Average-balance basis is not computed for 2025, because the 2024 closing trade-debt balance is not in this file.

The apparent improvement must be read with the composition in section 5. Over 90 days remains 77.7% of gross trade debts in 2026 (462,209,504 / 594,882,562), and related parties account for 98.2% of the over-90 bucket (453,876,337 / 462,209,504 gross general); non-related over-90 is 8,333,167 (1.8%).

## 7. Overdue-amount definitions (text) and reconciliation

The narrative amounts quoted in note 21.1 are **not total overdue trade debts**:

- Note 21.1 (PDF page 32; printed page not verified): "Trade debts include overdue amount of Rs 508,170 million (2025: Rs 549,976 million) **on account of inter-corporate circular debt**."
  - Total gross past-due (all non-current buckets) 2026 = 594,882,562 − 81,958,547 = **512,924,015**. Circular-debt overdue 508,170,000 is 99.1% of this.
  - Residual non-circular overdue = 512,924,015 − 508,170,000 = **4,754,015** (approximately; the 508,170 figure is in millions).
  - 2025: total past-due = 613,739,510 − 58,150,561 = 555,588,949; circular overdue 549,976 m; residual ≈ 5,613 m.
- Note 21.1 also states: circular-debt balance of Rs 251,435 m (2025: 264,208 m) and Rs 241,469 m (2025: 231,980 m) are overdue from **Sui Northern Gas Pipelines** and **Sui Southern Gas Company** respectively (both in millions).
  - Cross-check, SSGC: past due = 250,498,432 − 9,029,675 = **241,468,757** ≈ 241,469 m. ✔ Agrees.
  - Cross-check, SNGPL: past due = 271,775,195 − 20,171,996 = **251,603,199** vs 251,435 m stated. **Difference 168 m, unexplained.** Recorded; not forced.
- Circular-debt balance of Rs 101,012,655 (note 19.1, "net investment in lease") is a **lease receivable**, not the trade-debt table. The summary's "circular debt Rs 101bn" should be labelled "net investment in lease / circular-debt-related lease receivable" in later analysis.
- Note 19.2 (PDF page 32): current portion of net investment in lease billed to customers = Rs 10,648 m (2025: 33,654 m), of which **Rs 7,208 m (2025: 30,226 m) is overdue on account of inter-corporate circular debt**. Cross-check: lease ageing table (printed 279) gives billed total 10,648,055 = 3,440,096 + 2,661,261 + 2,664,269 + 1,882,429 (over 90: –). ✔ 2025: 33,654,455 = 3,427,993 + 2,649,925 + 2,645,838 + 2,645,838 + 22,284,861. ✔
