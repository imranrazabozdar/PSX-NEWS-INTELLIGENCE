# HUBC — Research Brief (Part 4: Street consensus & credit)

## Credit rating (PACRA) — strong, corroborates deleveraging
- PACRA maintains HUBC long-term entity rating **AA+**, short-term **A1+**, stable outlook (maintained 02-Jun-2026; unchanged since 2022). Second-highest rating band — very strong credit, consistent with the deleveraging and sound balance sheet seen in the statements.
- PACRA describes HUBC as a "diversified conglomerate" with a ~2,289 MW portfolio; core value in CPHGC (47.5%), plus Narowal, Laraib, TEL, ThalNova, CPHO (49%) and an 8% stake in SECMC (Thar coal mining). CPHGC's own rating rests on "contractual protections, tariff indexation" — supports the CPEC-protection thesis.
- Ownership: Mega Conglomerate ~19.48%, Fauji Foundation ~8.5% — strong institutional anchors.

## Street consensus — "Buy," but targets split around the current price
- S&P Global: 5-analyst consensus **"Buy," average target ~Rs202** (vs ~Rs230 mid-2026).
- MarketScreener: consensus **"Outperform," ~6 analysts, average target ~Rs184.56**.
- **Topline Pakistan (bull): DCF Dec-2026 TP Rs244** (+12% capital gain + 9.6% yield = ~22% total return), driven by BYD ramp, higher receivables recovery, and Thar-coal plant dividends.
- Older/other targets ranged Rs149–181 (earlier, more cautious).
- **Read:** cheap on multiples (P/E ~5.4x, P/B ~1.0x, yield ~9.5–10%), but analyst targets cluster *near or slightly below* the current price — the market already credits the risks. The bull case (Topline) depends on the *catalysts* (BYD, Thar dividends, receivables), not the base business. This matches my independent read: cheap, de-risking, catalyst-dependent.

## Extra optionality confirmed
- **Single Point Mooring (SPM) / oil-storage facility** planned on the Hub coast using freed base-plant land (Topline) — matches the CBS "oil-storage/aluminium smelter" land-repurposing.
- SECMC 8% (Thar coal mining) adds vertical integration into local fuel.
- BYD EV via Mega Motor Company; first locally assembled vehicle expected Q4FY26 after financial close Jan 2026.

## Full triangulation
Four independent layers now agree: (1) the audited statements, (2) sourced macro/policy, (3) broker research + management guidance (SCS), and (4) credit rating + Street consensus. All point to the same thing — **a cheap, strongly-rated, deleveraging power-investment holding company whose paper earnings are turning to cash, paying ~10%, with real EV/distribution/land optionality — priced for the risks (rate cycle, CPHGC tariff dispute, circular debt/receivables).** Consensus "Buy," but with target prices that say the easy money depends on the catalysts delivering.
